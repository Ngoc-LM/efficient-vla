\section{Efficient VLAs and Action Generation}
\label{sec:study}

\paragraph{Efficiency levers.}
\cref{tab:study} groups efficiency work by the part of the inference loop it changes.
Token and layer reduction assumes redundancy across frames~\cite{xu2026vla} or easy situations~\cite{yue2024deer} and saves compute only in the language model.
Chunked and asynchronous execution assumes that older actions stay valid; SmolVLA's asynchronous mode was 30\% faster but lowered success from 78.3\% to 73.3\%~\cite{shukor2025smolvla}.
Systems work assumes the model stays numerically the same, which an export can violate~\cite{islam2026faster}.
Two gaps remain: speed-ups come with aggregate success rates, though a non-significant difference is not equivalence, and most latencies with GPUs that have native bfloat16.

\paragraph{Diffusion versus flow matching.}
Both turn Gaussian noise $\boldsymbol\epsilon$ into an action chunk $\mathbf A$ conditioned on the observation $\mathbf o$.
Diffusion heads~\cite{chi2025diffusion,li2024cogact} noise the data as $\mathbf A_k=\sqrt{\bar\alpha_k}\mathbf A+\sqrt{1-\bar\alpha_k}\boldsymbol\epsilon$ and predict the noise~\cite{ho2020denoising}; flow-matching heads~\cite{black2024pi_0,shukor2025smolvla} interpolate linearly, $\mathbf A^\tau=\tau\boldsymbol\epsilon+(1-\tau)\mathbf A$, and regress the velocity of that path~\cite{lipman2022flow}:
\begin{align}
\mathcal L_\text{DDPM}&=\mathbb E\,\big\|\boldsymbol\epsilon-\boldsymbol\epsilon_\theta(\mathbf A_k,k\mid\mathbf o)\big\|^2,\\
\mathcal L_\text{FM}&=\mathbb E\,\big\|(\boldsymbol\epsilon-\mathbf A)-v_\theta(\mathbf A^\tau,\tau\mid\mathbf o)\big\|^2 .
\label{eq:fm}
\end{align}
They differ in four respects.
(i) Target: noise versus velocity.
(ii) Path: the linear path gives each training pair a constant velocity and yields straighter sampling trajectories~\cite{lipman2022flow}; on RoboTwin, $\pi_{0.5}$ keeps 71.3\% success with 3 flow steps against 71.5\% with 10, and drops to 59.8\% with one~\cite{tang2026fewer}.
(iii) Sampler: Diffusion Policy uses 100 DDPM steps in simulation and 10 DDIM~\cite{song2020denoising} steps on the robot, CogACT 10 DDIM steps with guidance, flow models 10 Euler steps.
(iv) Architecture: Diffusion Policy repeats its whole denoiser; CogACT, $\pi_0$ and SmolVLA run the VLM once and repeat only a head on its cached output.
