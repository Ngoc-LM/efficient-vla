\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

For the released SmolVLA checkpoint on a T4, the cost of a re-plan is set less by FLOPs than by emulated bfloat16 and a launch-bound flow loop.
Sharing computation across steps lowers success by about 12 points, whereas removing these overheads and the discretisation slack makes a re-plan $7.0\times$ cheaper without a measurable loss on 200 paired episodes.
