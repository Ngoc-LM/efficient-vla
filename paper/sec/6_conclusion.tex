\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

On a T4, the cost of a SmolVLA re-plan is set less by FLOPs than by emulated bfloat16 and a launch-bound flow loop.
Sharing computation across steps lowers success by about 12 points; removing these overheads and the discretisation slack with known levers makes a re-plan $7.1\times$ cheaper ($8.5\times$ with one Euler step) and stays non-inferior on paired episodes.
One noise per episode removes chatter and most successes, so chatter reads as a symptom.
\textbf{Limitations:} one checkpoint, suite and GPU type, in simulation; the 500-pair test was extended after an interim look; baseline 77.0\% against 90\% for the paper configuration.
\textbf{Future work:} a cheaper prefix (kernel fusion, token reuse or a distilled backbone) and the same paired closed-loop test on embedded GPUs and exported runtimes~\cite{islam2026faster}.
