\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

For the released LIBERO checkpoint of SmolVLA on a Tesla T4, the cost of a re-plan is set less by FLOPs than by two execution overheads: emulated bfloat16 arithmetic and the launch-bound flow loop of the action expert.
Sharing computation across control steps lowers success by about 12 points, whereas removing the two overheads and the discretisation slack reduces a re-plan from 789 to 112\,ms with bit-exact graph replay and unchanged success on 200 paired episodes.

\paragraph{Limitations.}
The study covers one checkpoint, one LIBERO suite and one GPU type, in simulation.
The reproduced baseline (77.0\%) is below the 90\% reported for the paper configuration of SmolVLA, which differs from the released checkpoint (\cref{sec:reproduce}).
The precision gain does not carry over to GPUs with bfloat16 tensor cores, and the step and graph gains were not measured on other hardware.

\paragraph{Future work.}
The remaining latency lies in the vision-language prefix (84 of 112\,ms).
Reducing it requires changing the model, for example distilling the policy into a shallower language model or a cheaper vision encoder, followed by the same paired closed-loop test.
Measurements on an embedded GPU and on exported runtimes (ONNX, TensorRT) should use the same test, since a faster runtime can change closed-loop behaviour~\cite{islam2026onnx}.
